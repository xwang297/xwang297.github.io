Hi, I am a Research Program Analyst at Johns Hopkins University, where I conduct program evaluation work. My research explores learner autonomy—how learners develop the capacity to take control of their own learning—with a focus on its measurement and on instructional strategies (engagement, motivation, and learning strategy instruction) that foster it. I am also interested in advancing research synthesis methods, including meta-analysis and systematic review, and in exploring how AI tools can improve the efficiency and reliability of this work.

**Previously:** I completed my Ph.D. in Educational Evaluation and Research at Johns Hopkins University's School of Education. I received my MA in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages from [The University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk). I was a research assistant at [The Education University of Hong Kong](https://www.eduhk.hk/en) from 2019-2021.
