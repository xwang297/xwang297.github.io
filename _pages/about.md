Hi, I am a 5th-year PhD candidate in the School of Education at Johns Hopkins University. My research interests include meta-analysis, learner autonomy, and program evaluation. I am currently working with [Dr. Marcia Davis](https://education.jhu.edu/directory/marcia-davis-phd) and [Dr. Amanda Neitzel](https://education.jhu.edu/directory/amanda-neitzel-inns-phd). I will be on the academic job market in 2025-2026.

**Previously:** I received my MA in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages from [The University of Hong Kong](https://www.hku.hk). I was a research assistant at [The Education University of Hong Kong](https://www.eduhk.hk/en).
